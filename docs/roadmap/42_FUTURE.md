# Purpose

This document defines the long-term roadmap and vision for Psychology AI Lab.

The ultimate goal is to establish the world's leading research platform for evaluating, improving, and validating AI-based psychological counseling systems.

Rather than focusing on a single AI model or provider, the platform aims to become a scientific laboratory where every aspect of AI counseling can be measured, compared, and continuously improved before deployment into production systems such as AIMindary.

---

# Vision

Psychology AI Lab seeks to answer a fundamental question

 How can we systematically improve AI counseling through scientific evaluation rather than intuition

Every enhancement—whether a model, prompt, persona, memory system, or knowledge base—should demonstrate measurable improvements before it is adopted.

# Summary

The Future Roadmap describes the long-term evolution of Psychology AI Lab from a benchmarking application into a comprehensive scientific research platform for AI counseling.

Beginning with baseline model evaluation and progressing through RAG, personas, memory systems, AI therapist architectures, and continuous learning, the platform is designed to ensure that every advancement is supported by rigorous experimentation and reproducible evidence. Through this research-first approach, Psychology AI Lab provides the scientific foundation upon which production systems such as AIMindary can safely and continuously evolve.
